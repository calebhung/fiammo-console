everything happening across all three surfaces, newest first — every HTTP call the iOS app makes to supabase, every edge function invocation with its status and duration, and every action taken on this page. click a row for the full record, and keep scrolling to pull in older history — it goes back 30 days. request and response bodies are never recorded, only their sizes.

⠿generate demo guest▾

      

      
creates a real, fully-onboarded account (profile + default circles) with a randomly generated email/password/phone that unmistakably signal it's a demo account. no posts, friends, or messages. optionally set the handle/first name/last name below — useful for copy accounts — anything left blank still gets a random demo value.

      

        
        
        
      

      

        
        
        
      

      

      

    


    

      
⠿change a handle▾

      

      
renames an account: the handle it has now, then the one it should have. sign-up's rules apply — 2–30 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and underscores — and a handle someone already holds is refused. the handle being left behind goes straight back on the shelf: anyone can sign up with it, and this tool will hand it to another account.

      

        
        
        
      

      

      

    


    

      
⠿delete user▾

      

      
permanently deletes a user and everything associated with them (posts, circles, messages, notifications, storage files) by email or username. this cannot be undone.

      

        
        
      

      

      

    


    

      
⠿refresh today's prompt▾

      

      
gives today a different prompt, effective immediately. it does this by swapping today's prompt with one about six months out — today shows that one, and today's original moves to that day. it does not skip forward: skipping made today borrow tomorrow's prompt, so the same prompt then appeared two days running. no other day changes, and the swap is permanent rather than a one-day override.

      
      

      

    


    

      
⠿send custom push notification▾

      

      
sends a push notification to every registered device, right now. this cannot be undone or recalled once sent — double-check the title and body below before confirming.

⠿refresh constellations▾

      

      
forces every user's constellation (mind-map themes + insight) to regenerate on their next app sync — bypasses the normal 7-day cooldown and minimum word/entry threshold.

      
      

      

    


    

      
⠿circle SMS▾

      

      
kill switch for the per-post Twilio texts to opted-in circle contacts. off by default — nothing sends, and no credits are spent, while disabled, no matter who posts.

⠿app config▾

      

      
values the app reads at runtime instead of having them baked into the build — change one here and it takes effect without an App Store release. every key also has a default compiled into the app equal to what's shown here, so a device that can't reach this table behaves exactly as it does today. the app caches these, so a change reaches a running device on its next launch or foreground, not instantly.

      

        
        
      

      

      

      

    


    

      
⠿daily prompts▾

      

      
what the app and the daily push will both show on a given day. step through dates to see what's coming — selection is pure arithmetic, so any date resolves, however far out. editing text is safe. pin to date takes over that one day and moves nothing else; adding to the rotation shifts every day including today, so it's for deliberate bulk changes only.


      

        
        
        
        
        
      


      

      


      

        
        
      

      

      

      

      

    


    

      
⠿label prompts▾

      

      
one prompt at a time: good or bad, and why. the next two months come first, in the order people will see them, then the rest of the library. a 👎 needs a reason, since that's what the judge learns from. the judge's own take stays hidden until you've labeled, so it can't lean on you.

⠿waitlist▾

      

      
with the waitlist on, every new account waits here after sign-up until it's let in. two ways in: a friend's invite seat, which approves them on its own (shown as seat), or approval here. oldest first. turning the waitlist off only lets new signups straight in; it never releases the people already waiting.

⠿reported posts▾

      

      
posts reported from the app, newest first. reporting hides the post for that reporter only — it's still live for everyone else unless you delete it or delete the author above. content shown is the live post, or a snapshot taken at report time if it's since been deleted.

      

        
        
      

      

      

    


    

      
⠿auto-flagged content▾

      

      
posts, comments, and messages that tripped the extreme-content keyword list (the auto_flag_terms table), newest first. flagging is report-only — nothing was hidden from anyone's feed or inbox. "mark reviewed" only clears the row from this list; deleting content or the author still happens through the tools above. if the author later edits the content and it still matches, the flag reopens.

      

        
        
        
      

      

      

    


    

      
⠿run full diagnostics▾

      

      
runs an end-to-end sweep of every backend-dependent feature (auth, circles, posts, echoes, DMs, blocking & reporting, notifications, push, schema drift) against two persistent diagnostic accounts, then cleans up everything it created. no real SMS/push reaches a device — pipelines are verified without contacting real phones. takes roughly 30–60s.
