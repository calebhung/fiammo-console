how fiammo is doing

Read from what the database keeps: accounts, friends, posts, journal entries, echoes, messages, groups and invites. It can't see app opens or screens, so being active here means doing something that leaves a row. Counts and sizes only; no post or message text reaches this page.

active people

daily, weekly and monthly active

How many people were active on each day, in the 7 days up to it, and in the 30 days up to it.

lifecycle

Each week: who joined, who stayed active from the week before, who came back after a quiet week, and who went quiet (below the line). The last week is still going.

how often people show up

People by the number of days they were active in the range.

activation and drop-off

activation funnel

People who joined in the range, and how far each got. Each step counts only people who also reached every step above it. Names under a step are the people who stopped there.

setting up

Of the people who joined in the range.

where people go quiet

People who joined in the range, joined at least 14 days ago and haven't been active in the last 14, grouped by the last thing they did.

retention

came back after

Of the people who joined in the range and have been around long enough, the share active again on or after day N.

weekly cohorts

People grouped by the week they joined, and the share active in each week after. Week 0 is their first seven days; joining alone doesn't count.

journeys

first steps

What people who joined in the range did first, second and third, counting each kind of thing once. Setting up (age, notifications) is left out.

time to first

How many people who joined in the range ever did each thing, and how long after joining they first did it.

engagement

feature adoption

Of the people who did anything in the range, how many used each part of the app.

activity by day

Each chart has its own scale; compare shapes, not heights.

when people write and message

Posts, taps and journal entries, and direct and group messages, by weekday and hour.

writing

Words split on spaces; characters leave spaces out, the way Constellation counts them, so writing without spaces between words still measures.

social

invites

Invites sent from the app and who joined through them. Only the earliest inviter of a number is credited.

group chats

Groups are numbered in the order they were made; names stay out of this page. Members don't count hidden test accounts; messages are in the range, of all time.

people

everyone

Counts are for the range; friends is who they're friends with now. Click a column to sort and a person to see their journey.

what this can't see

  • Opening the app, reading the feed, or looking at a profile. Release builds send no events, so someone who reads every day but never posts looks inactive here.
  • Anything deleted. A deleted post or message leaves its table; a burned post stays and counts.
  • When a friend request was accepted, where its notification is gone. The request time stands in.
